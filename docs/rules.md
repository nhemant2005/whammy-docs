# Hackathon Rules

## Schedule
- **Dates:** 23rd May - 24th May (2-Day Hackathon)
- **Submission Deadline:** 24th May, 11:59 PM IST

## Team Rules
- Choose any **1 problem statement** and build your solution
- Work individually or in teams of **max 2 members**

## Submission Requirements
- Project submission form will be shared on the WhatsApp group
- **Final submission must include:**
  - Project Demo Video (max 3 minutes)
  - GitHub Repository Link

## Project Guidelines
- Projects should be **functional MVPs/prototypes**
- **Bonus points for:**
  - Real-world usability
  - Good UI/UX
  - AI integration & automation
  - Creativity and technical implementation

## Important Notes
- Late submissions may not be considered for evaluation
- **Winners selected based on:**
  - Innovation
  - Technical execution
  - Problem-solving approach
  - Product thinking & usability
